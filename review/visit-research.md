## 2. Places to go, with the facts used

### Red Hill, Patrick Henry National Memorial (Brookneal)
- 1250 Red Hill Rd, Brookneal, VA 24528. Phone 434-376-2044.
- Hours: Mon–Sat 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Sun 1 to 5 p.m.; winter (Nov 1 to March) Mon–Sat 9 to 4, Sun 1 to 4. Closed New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas. [verify: footer and body disagree on when winter hours end, March 1 vs March 31]
- Admission (on arrival): adults $8, seniors $7, AAA/military/NPS pass $6, students 6–17 $4, EBT $2, children 5 and under free. [verify: prices change]
- On site: visitor center with orientation video, museum, Henry's law office, grave, nearly 1,000 acres. Congress designated it a National Memorial in 1986 (HISTORY, HMDB marker).
- Annual events listed by Red Hill and the county: Christmas Open House (Sunday, December 6, 2026, 1:30 to 4 p.m.); Fourth of July with fireworks (gates 4 p.m., fireworks about 9:15, admission fee); Naturalization Ceremony on Henry's birthday (May 29); Living History Days; star parties. [verify each year]
- Driving distance from Keysville: about 29 miles, roughly 45 minutes (OSRM routing; HISTORY says "about 25 miles southwest"). [verify: 25 vs 29 road miles]

Sources: https://www.redhill.org/plan-your-visit/ , https://www.redhill.org/ , county events calendar (below).

### Staunton River State Park (Scottsburg)
- 1170 Staunton Trail, Scottsburg, VA 24589. Phone 434-572-4623. Open 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Parking fee applies (amount not stated on the page). [verify fee]
- About 2,400 acres (page also says 2,336) on Buggs Island Lake (John H. Kerr Reservoir, 48,000 acres). More than 17 miles of multi-use trails. Cabins (7 or 8), a 14-person camping lodge, 48 campsites, equestrian campground with 13 sites and 20 covered stalls.
- First state park in Virginia designated an International Dark Sky Park (July 2015). Staunton River Star Party every March and October; Saturday nights of the event free and open to the public.
- As of October 2026 the page says "The pool will not open" and the Staunton River boat launch is closed for low water. [verify before publishing anything about the pool or launch]
- Distance from Keysville: about 34 miles, roughly 50 to 60 minutes.

Source: https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/staunton-river

### Staunton River Battlefield State Park (Randolph)
- 1035 Fort Hill Trail, Randolph, VA 23962. Phone 434-454-4312. Open 8 a.m. to dusk. Clover Visitor Center open Saturday and Sunday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. and has the park's only restroom.
- 300 acres. 1.2-mile self-guided battlefield trail and a 0.75-mile nature trail.
- June 25, 1864: about 700 Confederate "old men and young boys" held the railroad bridge against roughly 5,000 Union cavalry under Wilson and Kautz (county history page; park page; HISTORY).
- Distance from Keysville: about 21 miles, roughly 35 minutes.

Sources: https://dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/staunton-river-battlefield , https://www.charlottecountyva.gov/visitors/history.php

### Twin Lakes State Park (Green Bay, Prince Edward County)
- 788 Twin Lakes Rd, Green Bay, VA 23942. Phone 434-392-3435. Park hours 7 a.m. to dusk (page also says dawn to dusk). [verify]
- 548 acres; Prince Edward Lake (36 acres) and Goodwin Lake (15 acres). Unguarded swimming at Goodwin Lake year-round at no cost. Beach concession and boat rentals closed until Memorial Day weekend 2027. [verify seasonally]
- 11 climate-controlled cabins year-round, including Bowen Lodge (sleeps 16); 32 or 33 campsites; Cedar Crest Conference Center (largest room seats 135). Six miles of trails plus an 8-mile multi-use trail in the adjoining Prince Edward-Gallion State Forest.
- History: in 1948 M. Conrad Martin was denied entry to Staunton River State Park; the lawsuit led to Prince Edward State Park in 1950. "The two lake parks operated as racially segregated parks until the early 1960s." Merged 1976, renamed Twin Lakes 1986.
- Distance from Keysville: about 18 miles, roughly 25 to 30 minutes.

Source: https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/twin-lakes

### Briery Creek Lake and Wildlife Management Area (Prince Edward County)
- 845-acre DWR lake, about 7 miles south of Farmville off US 15 at Route 790; a second access on Route 701. Dam finished 1986, opened to fishing 1989.
- "One of the most important largemouth bass fisheries in the state." Also redear sunfish, bluegill, black crappie, channel catfish, chain pickerel. Standing and felled timber left for habitat (watch for it when paddling).
- Motors limited to 10 horsepower. [verify: page is inconsistent on the horsepower limit]
- DWR Access Permit required for visitors 17 and older unless they hold a Virginia hunting, freshwater fishing or trapping license, or a current Virginia boat registration.
- Distance from Keysville to the Route 790 ramp: about 11 miles, roughly 15 to 20 minutes.

Source: https://dwr.virginia.gov/waterbody/briery-creek-lake/

### Charlotte State Forest
- 5,688 acres; Saxkey Road trailhead reached from Keysville via VA 59 (Merry Oaks Dr / Saxkey Rd), about 9.7 miles. Walking and hiking free; State Forest Use Permit (16 and older) required for hunting, trapping, fishing, mountain biking and horseback riding. Hunting occurs; wear blaze orange or pink in season. Virginia Department of Forestry 434-995-8096. eBird lists 78 species.

Source: https://dwr.virginia.gov/vbwt/sites/charlotte-state-forest (the DOF page itself could not be fetched: https://dof.virginia.gov/state-forests/charlotte-state-forest/ was blocked).

### Tobacco Heritage Trail
- Built segments are in Brunswick, Mecklenburg and Halifax counties and in Victoria (Lunenburg): Brodnax to Lawrenceville 14.2 miles one way on crushed stone; La Crosse to Brodnax (paved); Boydton 1.1 miles; South Boston 3.9 miles; Victoria 2 miles. Open to walking, bicycles, strollers, wheelchairs; horse mounting benches on some sections.
- No segment is in Charlotte County or Keysville. In 2008 the town hoped to become a stop (Cooperative Living). Nearest segment: Victoria, about 18 miles east on VA 40. [verify current status of any Keysville extension]

Source: https://tobaccoheritagetrail.org/on-the-trail/

### Charlotte Court House
- County seat, 9 miles west on VA 40, population 505 (2020). Courthouse built 1821–1823 to plans supplied by Thomas Jefferson; NRHP 1980. Patrick Henry gave his last public speech there in March 1799, opposing the Virginia Resolutions, in the famous debate with John Randolph. Historic district on the National Register; county offers a walking-tour map. Museum of Charlotte County / Visitor's Center is there (Roanoke Station visitor center "currently under renovation").

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_County_Courthouse_(Virginia) , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Court_House,_Virginia , https://www.charlottecountyva.gov/visitors/historic_attractions/index.php , county history page.

### Southside Virginia Community College, John H. Daniel Campus
- 200 Daniel Road, Keysville, opened September 1971; Civil Rights in Education Heritage Trail marker on Daniel Road. About 2 miles from King Street. Hosts the county Relay for Life. (review/history-and-features.md; county events calendar.)

All from HISTORY / review/history-and-features.md.
- The depot: built 1890–1900, NRHP 2008, used in the 1976 TV film "Eleanor and Franklin." DHR now lists it under "Virginia's Lost": "By the early 2020's the Keysville Railroad Station was no longer extant," delisted 17 Sept 2026. The tour stop is written as the depot site, not a standing building. [verify locally what remains and whether a marker exists]
- Tower Park, an outdoor playground on Main Street in Keysville (county recreation page).
- About 70 businesses and "the largest restaurant selection in the county" (Cooperative Living 2008). [verify the claim is still true before using it]
